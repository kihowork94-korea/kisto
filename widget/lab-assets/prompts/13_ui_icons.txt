# 이모지를 교체해 게임 느낌을 더 올릴 수 있다 (이 교체는 코드 수정이 필요하니 넣어만 주면 된다).

# 크기: 각각 256 x 256 px, 투명 배경 PNG, 한 파일에 하나씩.

# 한 번에 만들려면 아래 [SHEET] 프롬프트로 5개를 한 장(1280x256, 가로로 나열)에 뽑은 뒤 잘라도 된다.

[STYLE]
2D game UI icon, hand-painted flat vector, soft cel shading, clean dark-brown outline
(#5b4027) about 6 px thick at 256 px, rounded chunky shapes, warm and friendly, readable
at 32 px. Palette: violet #7c3aed, mint #1baf7a, cream #fffdf8, sandy #d2b48c, walnut
#8b5e3c, charcoal #1f2937, terracotta #c2410c. Transparent background, single icon
centered, tiny soft shadow under the shape. No text, no letters, no numbers.

[quest_mark.png]
An exclamation-mark quest marker: a fat rounded exclamation mark in warm amber-gold with a
cream highlight, sitting inside a soft violet circular glow, slightly tilted, as if
floating. Reads instantly at small size.

[star_on.png]
A filled five-pointed achievement star in warm amber-gold with a cream inner highlight and
a thin darker gold rim, a few small sparkle dots around it.

[star_off.png]
The exact same five-pointed star in the identical shape and size, but unearned: flat pale
grey-beige fill (#d9d3c7) with the same outline, no sparkles, no highlight.

[level_badge.png]
A round laboratory level badge: a cream medallion with a walnut rim, a small violet conical
flask silhouette in the middle, two short ribbon tails in mint below, no numbers anywhere.

[stamp_clear.png]
A stage-clear rubber stamp mark: a rough circular ink ring in mint (#1baf7a) with a bold
checkmark inside, slightly rotated, with the uneven texture of a hand stamp on paper.

[SHEET]  (5개를 한 장에)
<위 [STYLE] 문단 전체>
A row of five separate game icons on a fully transparent background, evenly spaced, not
touching, identical style and lighting: (1) an amber exclamation-mark quest marker in a
violet glow, (2) a filled gold achievement star with sparkles, (3) the same star in flat
grey as an empty slot, (4) a round cream badge with a violet flask and mint ribbons,
(5) a mint circular stamp mark with a checkmark inside.

[NEGATIVE]
text, letters, numbers, watermark, signature, logo, photorealistic, 3D render, gradient
mesh, neon, dark background, white background, opaque background, drop shadow box, frame,
bevel, glossy plastic
